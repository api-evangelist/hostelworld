Brașov , Romania , is known for its medieval charm, mountain views and rich history. But inside one hostel on the city’s main pedestrian street, another story has been quietly unfolding. For more than a decade, Centrum House Hostel has helped young people gain experience, confidence and practical skills through hospitality.
